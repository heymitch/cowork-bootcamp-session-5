## Skills

| Skill | Say This | What Happens |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| **Vercel Landing Page** | "Build me a landing page for X" | Describe your offer and your agent writes the copy (hero, problem, solution, features, pricing, CTA), generates the code, shows you a preview, and deploys it live to a URL. Page is on the internet in under a minute. Pulls from your business context and voice profile if available. |
| **Frontend Design** | "Design a page for X" | When you want something that doesn't look like generic AI output. This skill commits to a bold aesthetic direction — brutalist, editorial, retro-futuristic, luxury, whatever fits — and builds production-grade code with distinctive typography, intentional color, motion, and spatial composition. The opposite of cookie-cutter. |

## Session Order

Total time: ~30 minutes.

1. **Vercel Landing Page** — describe your offer and watch it go live *(15 minutes)*
2. **Frontend Design** — level up the design or build a standalone page with real aesthetic direction *(15 minutes)*

Vercel Landing Page gets you from zero to live URL fast. Frontend Design is for when "fast" isn't enough and you want something people remember. Use them together: Landing Page for speed, Frontend Design for polish.

After this session, you can ship a live page for any offer in minutes. Session 6 teaches you to build your own skills.
