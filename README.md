# Digital Product

Build a digital product from idea to live sale — find ideas, validate demand, outline with frameworks, write the product, design it, and launch with multi-platform promotion.

## Install

Upload the ZIP via **Plugins > Upload Plugin** in Claude Cowork.

## What You Get

Install this plugin and 7 new capabilities appear in your `/` menu:

| Skill | Say This | What Happens |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| **Idea Finder** | "What should I build?" | Scans your existing content for demand signals — comments, engagement, recurring topics. Presents 3 evidence-based product candidates with working titles. |
| **Topic Validator** | "Test this product idea" | Generates 3 social posts (teach, ask, contrarian) to validate demand before you build anything. Explains what signals to watch for after posting. |
| **Outline Builder** | "Run the 8-question framework" | Interactive interview — asks 8 questions one at a time to structure your product: audience, problem, transformation, myths, origin story, proof, and chapters. |
| **Title Generator** | "Name my product" | Generates 5 titles using the 1+1+1 framework (product + audience + problem). Each labeled by style — direct, aspirational, contrarian, numbered, story-based. Recommends one. |
| **Content Writer** | "Write the ebook" | Expands your outline into a complete product using the 10 Proven Sections template. Confirms scope first (mini guide, standard, or premium) then writes section by section. |
| **Design Advisor** | "Design my product cover" | Recommends design direction matched to your budget — DIY with Canva, Fiverr freelancer with ready-to-send outreach, or professional designer. Generates a design brief either way. |
| **Launch Kit** | "Launch my product" | Generates pricing recommendation, sales page copy, platform setup steps (Gumroad/Stripe/Lemon Squeezy), 3 launch day posts, 3 evergreen promotion templates, and a personalized launch checklist. |

The **router** runs invisibly in the background — say "build a digital product" and it chains all 7 skills in sequence from idea to launch.

## Suggested Order (First Time)

1. **Idea Finder** — scan your content for what people already care about *(5 min)*
2. **Topic Validator** — post 3 test posts and wait a week for signals *(5 min + 1 week)*
3. **Outline Builder** — run the 8-question framework interview *(15 min)*
4. **Title Generator** — name your product *(5 min)*
5. **Content Writer** — write the full product *(30-60 min)*
6. **Design Advisor** — get cover design direction *(5 min)*
7. **Launch Kit** — generate everything to go live *(10 min)*

## Reference Material

The plugin includes reference files that the skills consult automatically:

- **LTL Frameworks** — The 8-question product framework, 1+1+1 naming formula, and 10 Proven Sections template for writing the full product
- **Pricing Guide** — Decision tree for pricing digital products by type, audience size, and problem severity
- **Launch Checklist** — Pre-launch, launch day, and post-launch action items
- **Platform Guides** — Setup walkthroughs for Gumroad, Stripe, and Lemon Squeezy
